## 📋 Operations & Processes

### Daily Standup (15 minutes)
```
Format: 9:00 AM Daily
Participants: All staff
Topics:
- What did you complete yesterday?
- What are you working on today?
- Any blockers?

Goal: Alignment & transparency
```

### Weekly Planning (1 hour)
```
Day: Monday 10:00 AM
Participants: Team leads
Agenda:
- Week overview
- Priority tasks
- Resource allocation
- Risk/issues

Output: Weekly priorities board
```

### Bi-weekly Retrospective (1 hour)
```
Schedule: Every 2 weeks (Friday)
Participants: All staff
Topics:
- What went well?
- What could improve?
- Action items

Goal: Continuous improvement
```

### Monthly All-Hands (1.5 hours)
```
Schedule: Last Friday of month
Agenda:
- Company metrics review
- Goal progress review
- Department updates
- Team recognition
- Q&A session

Goal: Alignment & celebrate wins
```

### Quarterly Business Review (2 hours)
```
Schedule: End of each quarter
Participants: Leadership + team
Agenda:
- Quarter results review
- Goals achievement
- Next quarter planning
- Strategic initiatives
- Budget review

Goal: Strategic alignment
```
